## Implemented behavior

- A small conversation working buffer retains authenticated instruction version and stable account/pursuit/task/source references for consecutive same-account turns. It is conversational state, not a separate process or persistent Android filesystem. Instructions require a new episode on account change, reconnect/access failure, policy refresh or ten-minute expiry. Fresh chats retrieve durable Business OS state; routine current-state reports and CRM projections still read live provider records. Authentication and consequential review remain host-enforced on every operation.
- Brief account context returns latest interpretations and current CRM records without repeating historical CRM JSON or full original source bodies. History and originals remain retrievable. Independent provider collections are fetched in parallel; persistence stays sequential.
- One typed capture call preserves the exact executive statement, attributes it to the authenticated actor, links interpretations, and returns a compact receipt. Corrections reuse stable entry IDs and preserve all prior source lineage. Input deduplication prevents a retried old capture from reverting a newer interpretation.
- Targeted email review can retrieve a unique authorized thread and its participants in one call. Native escaped subject/thread/message filters narrow provider reads. Ambiguous or incomplete discovery is explicit; per-collection pagination remains bounded and does not claim full mailbox synchronization. Original message text and participant records are retained with references.
- Instructions prioritize track/report/surface, brief operational replies, quiet remember/correction acknowledgements, no needless contact-enrichment questions, and no invented current sending status from historical statements or one thread's missing attachment.
