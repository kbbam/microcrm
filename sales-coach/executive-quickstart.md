# Coach: connect and try it on your phone

Use your company Claude account. Kirill supplies your personal Business OS activation link and enables your sales pilot.

1. Open your activation link and set your Business OS password. Then open [Claude connectors](https://claude.ai/customize/connectors/yours), choose **Coach → Connect**, sign in with your own Business OS work email, and press **Allow connection**. Use the same company Claude account if the browser asks you to sign in to Claude.
2. Open [Twenty accounts](https://cureous.twenty.com/settings/accounts) and connect your work Google account. In [email settings](https://cureous.twenty.com/settings/accounts/emails), set **Import: Everything; Visibility: Everything; Contact auto-creation: Sent and Received; Exclude group emails: On; Exclude non-professional emails: Off**. Enable calendar sync. Return to [Business OS source status](https://business-os-production-1193.up.railway.app/account/twenty/status), connect Twenty as yourself if requested, then press **Recheck connection**.
3. In Claude on your **iPhone or Android**, start a normal chat: **“@Coach set up. Our organization configured Coach’s internal tools as Always allow; verify my connection and show my assigned and available sales work. Then read [known email thread] and [known calendar meeting] from Twenty.”** If an approval appears, open [Claude connectors](https://claude.ai/customize/connectors/yours) → **Coach → Tool permissions → Other tools** menu → **Always allow**. Skip this if already selected; settings save immediately. Report any locked setting to Kirill.
4. Dictate one real note: **“@Coach, for [account], remember: [one real update].”** Wait for confirmation. Start a **new chat**: **“@Coach, what was my latest update for [account]?”** The update must match, with no repeated approval. Open that chat on your computer: the same conversation should be there.
5. Attach one ordinary screenshot/document **once**: **“@Coach, save the original and capture the useful information for [account]. Tell me whether the original was stored.”** In a new chat ask: **“@Coach, show the information and original file I last attached for [account].”** The link must open. Tell Kirill your phone type, approximate wait, and any failed step/prompt. Do not send passwords.

[MCP address, only if Coach is missing](https://business-os-production-1193.up.railway.app/coach/mcp). Ask Kirill to enable it; do not create a second QA connector.

To pick up work: **“@Coach, show available sales work. Assign [named responsibility] to me.”** To hand it off: **“@Coach, assign my [named opportunity/task] to [colleague].”** The coach must name the exact responsibility changed. An empty available list means the pilot has no eligible work yet; ask Kirill to add the initial customer list, or create a new account with the coach.
