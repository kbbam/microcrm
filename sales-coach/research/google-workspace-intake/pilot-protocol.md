## Evidence format and verdict

For each run retain route, actor/source identity, app/device version, model/effort, prompt, UTC submission time, source retrieval/version time, first-useful/final elapsed time, tool names/counts, returned IDs/counts/text hashes, participant-role match, pagination/truncation, attachments actually acquired and exact errors/approval pauses. Record no guessed tool timings or hashes.

Coverage pass means all independently verified fixture messages/participants and source distinctions survive. A successful search alone is not a coverage pass. Time comparisons use matched no-prompt trials and disclose all samples; data freshness requires a known source update timestamp. Research verdict may be insufficient evidence if grants/fixtures are missing.

When integration is later approved, a separate capture/revocation test must prove source text/metadata reaches BOS, is available to permitted supervisor agents, survives chat deletion, and cannot be retrieved by an unauthorized actor. The current read-only experiment cannot prove that contract.
